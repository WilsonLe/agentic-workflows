# Onboarding and ingress

1. Confirm the absolute project root, artifact root, and `tracked`, `ignored`, or
   `mixed` Git policy.
2. Create a versioned audience profile that keeps requested demographics
   separate from observed source coverage.
3. Register sources as read-only adapters. Record class, auth mode,
   credential-reference name, markets, languages, freshness, terms, retention,
   personal-data policy, and limitations.
4. Test a bounded sample, normalize it, and enable the source explicitly.
5. Prefer offline CSV, JSON, NDJSON, and RSS fixtures. Catalog credentialed or
   licensed adapters without claiming they are installed.
6. Treat editorial attention, public charts, licensed listening, marketplace
   saturation, and first-party commerce as different signal families.
7. Preserve missing, stale, conflicting, and negative evidence.

Never infer a Ho Chi Minh City 18–28 FYP from Vietnamese-language or national
content. Never activate automation during setup.
