# Extraction and appraisal

Pilot and version the extraction form. Each value records its form version, report/study, data
item, value, unit, source locator, extractor, contributor type, timestamp, transformation status,
and author-contact status. Preserve independent entries where the method requires them, then record
conflicts and resolutions without overwriting either entry. Log every calculation, conversion,
imputation, mapping, or coding transformation with input, rule/code, output, and verifier.

Select appraisal or risk-of-bias instruments by study design, review question, and current method
guidance. Record instrument name, version, authoritative source, licence, applicability, assessor,
domain judgment, support, locator, conflict, and resolution. Restricted instruments are linked,
not copied.

Reporting quality is not risk of bias. Do not convert domains into an unqualified universal
numerical quality score. An overall judgment is allowed only when the chosen instrument defines it,
and its derivation must remain instrument-specific and traceable. Design/instrument mismatch,
missing support, unresolved assessor conflict, or absent required human expertise blocks synthesis.
