# CYOBot reference observations

Inspection date: 2026-10-06. Evidence consists of live browser inspection of the public site and authenticated student, instructor, and manager views in an available session. This record keeps generalized design observations only. Authenticated URLs may require access, and the product can change after inspection.

## Stated preference versus observed design

Wilson identified CYOBot's macro decisions as close to his taste and explicitly preferred blank, simple, minimal defaults, purposeful information, and gradual disclosure through user interaction. Those statements are the taste authority. CYOBot is an example from which useful patterns can be inferred; it is not proof that every part of the product is minimal or that its styling should be copied.

## Observed surfaces

| Surface | Observed behavior | Transferable interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Public homepage](https://cyobot.com/) | Spacious light hero, concise purpose, a Start Quiz action, and product imagery | Establish purpose and a next step before exhaustive explanation |
| Homepage level selection | Selecting Explorer, Adventurer, or Pioneer changes the relevant explanation, progress, and projects | Present the chosen context instead of every variant simultaneously |
| Homepage hardware/environment sections | Hardware descriptions expand on request; the chosen environment determines displayed content | Defer supporting explanation and show contextually relevant material |
| [Quiz](https://cyobot.com/quiz) | Intro with Start quiz, followed by one question with progress and next/back controls | Guide a dependent sequence while retaining orientation |
| [Student dashboard](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/student) | Minimal account/workspace controls; progress, career/skill map, quests, rewards, skills, and shortcuts in a playful dark presentation | Select a learner's content vocabulary and controls by role |
| Student streak detail | A streak control reveals active/best streak information on demand | Supplementary detail can follow explicit interest |
| [Student role gallery](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/student/role-gallery) | Image/title choices open a detail modal with work characteristics, toolkit, fields, and statistics; Select role is a separate action | Separate browsing and understanding from commitment |
| [Instructor dashboard](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/instructor) | Spacious light work area, dark sidebar, workspace/quest routes, teaching resources, and coding tools | Organize a teaching workspace around useful routes |
| [Quests list](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/quests) | Search, archived/column controls, and a table; bulk archive appears after row selection | Keep frequent scanning tools accessible and selection actions contextual |
| Quest editor | Saved draft status, expandable description/objectives/levels, and secondary publishing/version controls | Disclose authoring detail by section and communicate work state |
| [Manager dashboard](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/managers) | Member and quest counts link to detailed pages; settings/workspace routes and a Members navigation entry | Summaries should route to actionable records |
| [Members](https://cyobot.com/dashboard/members) | Search, invite control, paginated records with roles/status/actions; History opens a dialog | Keep routine operations direct and audit detail on demand |

## Evidence limits

The inspected student workspace had little published learning content. The presence of dashboard sections was verified; populated lesson execution, populated rewards, and the full skill-map journey were not. The student dashboard has several fixed panels and expressive styling, so it is not evidence for a universally empty white dashboard.

The quiz's initial question and controls were inspected without completing a submission. Role detail was viewed without selecting a role. Member history was inspected without inviting or modifying members. Authoring observations establish the editor's visible structure and draft status, not a completed publication workflow.

These observations do not establish accessibility, mobile behavior, server authorization, or usability metrics. Recommendations about those areas come from the skill's design judgment and must be verified in the target implementation. No account identifiers, workspace values, screenshots, or brand assets are included.

## How to use this reference

Borrow the reasons: context changes content, detail follows interest, actions follow selection, role affects navigation, and summaries lead to work. Design the target product around its own user's task. Validate a borrowed pattern when its cost, frequency, or information needs differ from the observed example.
