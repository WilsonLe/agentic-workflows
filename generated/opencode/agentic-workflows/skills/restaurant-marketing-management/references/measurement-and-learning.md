# Measurement and learning

Choose a business outcome as the primary KPI: incremental item contribution, qualified bookings,
redemptions with contribution, covers in the target period, repeat purchase, or another outcome tied
to the campaign objective. Reach, impressions, views, likes, and engagement are diagnostics, not the
primary result.

Define before launch:

- a stable campaign ID and lowercase non-personal UTM fields;
- baseline period and known comparability limits;
- POS item code, offer code, booking source, landing event, call source, or manual tally;
- primary KPI, diagnostic metrics, fulfillment and margin guardrails;
- monitoring owner, cadence, pause conditions, end date, and decision date.

At close, separate:

- `observations`: values read from named sources and periods;
- `calculations`: formulas and explicit assumptions;
- `inferences`: interpretations with a stated confidence and basis;
- `unknowns`: missing or ambiguous evidence;
- `decisions`: stop, repeat, revise, scale, or run a cleaner test.

Do not claim attribution or incrementality from correlation alone. Check cannibalization, stockouts,
capacity failures, seasonality, calendar differences, channel overlap, and data gaps. Verify that
expired offers, links, ads, posts, menus, and staff instructions were removed or updated.
