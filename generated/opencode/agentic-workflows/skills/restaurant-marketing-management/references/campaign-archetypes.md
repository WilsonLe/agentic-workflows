# Campaign archetypes

Choose the smallest primary archetype that matches the business problem.

| Archetype | Primary job | Required extra checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `new_dish` | Establish awareness and trial for a permanent item | recipe truth, availability, staff explanation, item-level sales baseline |
| `offer` | Change demand with a price, bundle, or benefit | contribution economics, terms, eligibility, redemption, downside cap |
| `seasonal_dish` | Sell a genuinely time- or supply-limited item | source of seasonality, supply uncertainty, truthful scarcity, expiry |
| `event` | Fill a dated experience or catering occasion | capacity, booking path, cancellation terms, service plan |
| `slow_period` | Shift demand into a weak day or daypart | baseline by period, cannibalization risk, staffing and margin |
| `local_discovery` | Improve findability and conversion on owned/local surfaces | listing truth, hours, menu and destination consistency |
| `reputation` | Encourage policy-compliant feedback or recover a guest | no gating or sentiment incentives, response escalation |
| `retention` | Increase lawful return visits or win-back | consent, suppression, frequency, cohort and incremental measurement |
| `opening_relaunch` | Explain a new or materially changed restaurant proposition | operational readiness, hours, capacity, routing, phased launch |

Do not combine archetypes merely to make a campaign look comprehensive. Link a secondary tactic only
when it has a distinct outcome, owner, approval, and measurement path.
