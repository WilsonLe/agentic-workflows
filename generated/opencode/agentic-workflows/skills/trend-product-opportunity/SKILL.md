# Trend Product Opportunity

Read [product reasoning](references/workflow.md). Never map a trend directly to a
product. Extract reusable trend grammar while excluding protected expression.
Create three materially different hypotheses, identify buyer job and occasion,
select the physical product for a stated reason, model costs and speed, record
negative evidence, and define the cheapest test plus kill conditions.
