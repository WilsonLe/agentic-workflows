# Search opportunity research

Read for query discovery, prioritization and content/utility briefs. Refresh
[primary guidance](source-basis.md) when making platform-specific decisions.

## Find the moment before the product is needed

Describe the job in the customer's language. Generate specific task + constraint
queries: input format, output requirement, audience, device, integration, deadline,
error, alternative or purchase decision. Cover learning, comparing and ready-to-act
intent; explain the next step that makes the product relevant.

A photo-restoration product might explore damaged scan repair, print preparation
and old-image colorization. These are candidate directions until researched.
Do not infer commercial demand or easy rankings from a plausible phrase.

Use existing Search Console queries and landing pages when available, customer
questions, bounded public forum observations, autocomplete and related questions,
then current results and trend evidence. Preserve exact observed phrases and
permalinks without collecting identities. Generated variants remain candidates.
Use the available Reddit browsing skill for Reddit research; source-access limits
must survive the handoff. No tool availability is implied by the source post.

## Record observations, then interpret

For each query retain:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Query and provenance | Exact phrase, observed or generated, source URL/export and capture date |
| Context | Country/region, language, device, search surface and time range, or unknown |
| Intent and product fit | Learning/comparing/ready to act; job, supported capability and CTA |
| Demand | Trends/first-party evidence with settings, seasonal timing; unknown where unavailable |
| Results | Relevant ranking URLs, content types, dates and concrete unmet need observed |
| AI surface | Present, not observed in this sample, or unknown; cited links if actually visible |
| Asset | Existing URL to improve or proposed format/URL, cluster and distinguishing contribution |
| Decision | Priority rationale, effort, confidence, blocker and next validation |

Inspect leading relevant results for shortlisted queries with a stated bound
(for example, the first five organic results per finalist). Record ads separately.
If using a third-party index rather than live Google results, name it; do not infer
Google rankings, People Also Ask or AI Overviews from that index's absence/presence.
Do not call competition weak just because results are forums or small sites.
Demonstrate a gap: a missing worked example, unsupported input, unanswered
constraint, obsolete interface or unfulfilled job. Include strong incumbent
coverage as counterevidence and reject queries without a credible contribution.

Google Trends is relative, sampled interest under selected settings, not absolute
search volume; a zero for a narrow phrase does not establish zero demand.
Autocomplete and related questions suggest language, not measured volume or
purchase intent. Record topic versus term and compare matching settings. For
seasonal topics, account for lead time and historical recurrence. A single spike
does not establish a durable opportunity. If unavailable, use corroborating
evidence and mark trend status unknown; evergreen intent can still qualify.

AI search features vary by query and context. Observe them only when the chosen
surface exposes them. Suggest clear answers, original evidence and useful
demonstrations without claiming to know a recipe for citations. Google's current
guidance applies normal search eligibility and SEO practices to these features;
verify that guidance rather than inventing special markup requirements.

## Prioritize and map assets

Eliminate unsupported product promises and topics without a plausible audience
path first. Then compare finalists using proximity to the job, observed gap,
credible demand, distinct contribution, conversion path and total effort.
An explained ordinal ranking is sufficient. Keep confidence separate from value;
a high-value unverified idea is research work, not a publication-ready item.

Cluster by shared intent, not wording alone. Reuse or improve an existing page
when it already owns that job. Create a separate page only for a distinct useful
answer; prevent multiple pages competing for the same task. Keep rejected/deferred
queries and their reasons in the backlog so the next cycle need not rediscover them.

End with up to five priorities: query, intent, evidence/gap, asset, first-paragraph
question, product bridge/CTA, effort, confidence and next action. Create briefs only
for the batch capacity supports; twenty candidate queries do not mandate twenty
articles. If validation is unavailable, label the shortlist provisional.

## Produce a useful asset

Lead with the answer to the actual question. Specify original examples, source
links, calculations or demonstrations, factual claim checks, screenshots needed,
limitations and a next step the product truly supports. A comparison needs current
verifiable criteria and an honest account of alternatives. A utility needs a
working result and tested inputs/failure behavior, not a disguised signup wall.

For publication handoff, include unique title and description, URL/canonical
intent, crawl/index eligibility, descriptive internal links, usable mobile layout,
accessible content and a verified CTA/event path. Route fixes through the existing
site workflow. Do not recommend bulk thin pages, copied competitor text, doorway
pages, artificial engagement or fabricated examples/results as customer evidence.
