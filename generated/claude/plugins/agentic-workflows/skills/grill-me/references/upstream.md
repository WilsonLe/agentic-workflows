- [MIT license](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/LICENSE)

The upstream entry point delegates to `grilling`. This package combines that entry
point and method into one self-contained skill. It preserves the decision tree,
dependency ordering, recommended answers, fact-finding, and shared-understanding
goal. The adaptation favors one focused question or a small independent round,
uses available host tools, makes delegation optional, persists decisions in the
calling workflow's existing records, and respects already-settled authorization
instead of requiring a repeated final confirmation in every SDLC task.
