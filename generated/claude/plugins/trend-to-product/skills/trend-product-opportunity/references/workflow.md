# Product reasoning

Record trend thesis, buyer/audience revision, job, occasion, identity/emotional
signal, reusable grammar, excluded protected expression, product fit, visual
hook, copy strategy, price/cost/margin assumptions, manufacturing, fulfilment,
trend window, competition, evidence, hypothesis, next test, and expiry.

Product-fit weights: audience resonance 15, recognition 15, product context 15,
commercial gap 15, margin 10, manufacturability 10, speed 10, extensibility 5,
testability 5. Unknown rights, cost, margin, or fulfilment blocks the candidate.
Rights, likeness, sensitive-topic, factual-claim, and feasibility failures
reject it regardless of points. Below 70 do not generate final artwork; 70–79
allows a cheap concept test; 80+ permits approved design exploration.
