# Account and team management

Use the [Codex connected-app contract](../SKILL.md). Discover current read tools
for the authenticated identity, teams, projects, memberships, domains and spend.
Verify identity and exact team/project IDs before dependent operations. Follow
pagination and reconcile conflicting inventory rather than selecting the first
matching team name. Summarize only relevant roles, limits and resource metadata.

For an authorized membership, role, transfer, domain, integration or billing
change, inspect the exact exposed tool schema and required permissions. Capture
the previous state, intended destination, access/cost effect and recovery. Retain
a usable owner path for transfers or removals. Sending invitations or other
messages requires explicit communication authority.

Execute through the connected app and re-read saved state and effective access.
An absent tool or permission remains a blocker; do not fall back to the CLI,
REST calls or dashboard. State when a change has no automatic rollback.
