# Site-specific checks

Load the applicable sections. Keep one audit record and shared finding IDs rather
than separate reports with conflicting business facts or priorities.

## Local business

Compare the public website, supplied Business Profile evidence and sampled
listings for the actual name, address/service area, phone, hours, category,
services/menu, booking/ordering destination and location identity. Mark owner-only
verification/status fields unknown without authorized account evidence. Do not
invent locations, service areas, awards or customer reviews.

Inspect useful location/service pages and truthful business markup. A missing
street address is not automatically a defect for an eligible service-area
business. Verify current profile eligibility/rules before recommending changes.
For map/local observations retain query, search coordinates/area, date, device
and result context; one search cannot establish coverage across a city. A
geo-grid or provider sample needs a declared area, budget and available tools.

Use [Google's local guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).
Rankings vary with relevance, distance and prominence; profile completeness
cannot guarantee a map position. Review public review themes without fabricating,
gating or incentivizing positive reviews. `restaurant-marketing-management`
retains campaign truth, economics, permissions and any live review response.

## Ecommerce

Inspect representative product, variant and collection templates, crawlable
pagination, faceted/filter URLs, canonical choices and out-of-stock behavior.
Evaluate whether a filter serves a distinct useful search task before proposing
indexation; avoid blanket noindex/canonical rules that hide valuable inventory.
Verify title, description, images, shipping/returns, actual price/currency,
availability and structured data against the visible offer. Inspect the product
selection and purchase journey only within authorized actions; an audit is not
authority to submit an order or change a price/feed.

Distinguish on-page markup, authorized Merchant Center/feed evidence, rich-result
eligibility and observed listing appearance. Missing account data stays unknown.
Current feature rules belong to
[Google's ecommerce guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce)
and the relevant product feature documentation; marketplace and paid-provider
data are optional, with their source, market and coverage retained.

## International and multilingual

Build a small matrix of equivalent pages, actual language/region, canonical,
status, index intent and hreflang destinations. Inspect self and reciprocal
alternate references, fully qualified reachable URLs and valid language/script/
region codes. `x-default` is a conditional fallback, not a required language.
Check translation and region-specific product/price/claim parity with appropriate
review; a tag alone does not demonstrate localization quality.

Reconcile canonicals with the intended locale variants rather than canonicalizing
all translations to one language. For duplicate same-language regional pages,
verify the chosen strategy using the actual content and
[Google's localized-version guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions).
Inspect redirects/selectors for accessible alternatives; do not force every
visitor/crawler into an IP-derived locale.

## Programmatic, location and comparison pages

Map the source data, template, intended audience task and unique useful result.
Sample populated, sparse, duplicate and invalid combinations before recommending
expansion. Confirm a maintained inventory and canonical/internal-link plan;
large URL counts alone establish neither spam nor useful coverage.

Propose a small pilot with acceptance based on factual usefulness, data quality,
working journeys, index observations and qualified outcomes. Avoid arbitrary
minimum words, uniqueness percentages or mass keyword/location page creation.
Apply current [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
to the actual behavior. Page generation, deletion, redirects and publication
retain their implementation and release authority.
