# Measurement, prioritization and recheck

## Evidence coverage

Record what each channel can support before interpreting results:

| Channel | Supports | Does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source files/export | Supplied implementation at a stated revision/date | Current deployed response or search behavior |
| HTTP response and rendered browser | Observed access, markup and user journey | Engine-selected canonical, indexing or rankings |
| Authorized Search Console/export | Reported search/inspection state for its property, filters and dates | Complete query demand or immediate live state |
| CrUX or permitted real-user data | Field distribution for its URL/origin, device and window | Every page/device or a change's causal effect |
| Lab performance test | Reproducible diagnostics under stated conditions | A field pass or actual field INP from load-only TBT |
| Search/AI answer sample | Observed result under recorded conditions | Exhaustive ranking, citation share or referrals |
| Analytics/product/commerce data | Recorded events under its definitions and consent/attribution limits | All users, incremental revenue or a causal SEO lift |
| Backlink export/public sample | Links covered by that source/date and its collection method | A complete live profile, a penalty or authority score from Google |

Keep provider definitions, privacy filters, sampling, time zones, date ranges,
property/URL scope and known delays. Empty, withheld, unavailable and measured
zero are different states. Do not add incompatible metrics or infer conversions
from impressions. A vendor difficulty/authority/spam score is a vendor metric;
do not recommend disavow or outreach from it alone.

## Action plan

For every recommendation link the observed cause, intended effect, affected
reach, confidence, dependencies, owner, cost/effort assumptions and acceptance
check. Severity describes the supported consequence:

- Critical: observed broad availability/indexing failure for pages intended to
  be public, or a verified severe failure of the main user task.
- High: demonstrated material issue on an important template/journey.
- Medium/Low: bounded improvement or enhancement with stated expected value.
- Unassessed: evidence missing; specify the next check without assigning failure.

Choose urgency from the actual impact and business calendar, not a fixed universal
deadline. A canonical/content fix may need its dependent redirect/link/sitemap
updates in one implementation; another generic schema block is rarely a remedy
for an unavailable page. Treat proposed benefit as a hypothesis. No aggregate
score, passing validator or count of resolved warnings proves SEO health.

## Baseline and outcome review

Before authorized changes, preserve the selected URLs/templates, revision,
fetch/render conditions, status/robots/canonical/metadata/schema evidence,
available field/lab measures and relevant search/business baseline. Choose a
review window and success/failure checks appropriate to traffic, seasonality,
deployment time, data lag and expected effect. Do not invent an earlier baseline
when none exists; create one and leave historical comparison unavailable.

Afterward compare like with like, retaining changed inputs and evidence dates.
Record the actual deployment/URL and rerun direct acceptance checks first.
Then inspect search-engine processing and outcomes when data becomes available;
no fixed wait guarantees indexing. Keep technical completion, observed indexing,
search visibility, qualified actions, revenue and retained customers separate.
Uncontrolled before/after traffic changes support an association, not a causal
claim; note other releases, campaigns, demand and measurement changes.

Resume from the same baseline/finding IDs and retain the original values. A new
deployment or changed sampling/conditions can invalidate a comparison; explain
and establish a compatible baseline rather than silently overwrite history.
Return fixed, still observed, regressed or unassessed per finding, together with
the next action and owner. Recurring monitoring needs its own requested schedule;
this skill does not install a daemon, database or background job.
