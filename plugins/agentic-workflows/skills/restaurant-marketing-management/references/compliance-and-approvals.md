# Compliance and approvals

This workflow is operational guidance, not legal advice. Resolve the campaign jurisdiction and
refresh current regulator and platform guidance before live action.

## Approval model

Keep permissions narrow and auditable. Record the action, exact target, scope, status, approver, and
spend cap if relevant. Distinguish:

- approval of facts and claims;
- approval of final creative;
- approval to publish or schedule an exact deliverable;
- approval to send to a defined consented audience;
- approval to activate an offer or change a menu price;
- approval of a bounded ad spend;
- approval of an influencer agreement;
- approval of a sensitive public review response.

Draft approval does not imply any later action. Expired or rejected approval is not reusable.

## Policy invariants

- Direct marketing needs a current lawful basis, identifiable sender, working unsubscribe, and
  suppression process.
- Influencer relationships require honest opinion and conspicuous disclosure of material connection.
- Alcohol campaigns require jurisdiction-specific advertising and responsible-service review.
- Testimonials and reviews must be real and accurately represented.
- Never fake reviews, gate review requests by sentiment, reward only positive sentiment, or offer
  value for revision or removal.
- Never publish unsupported savings, scarcity, health, dietary, allergen, provenance, award, or
  popularity claims.

After an authorized mutation, read the real public or account surface back and compare it with the
approved target. Record discrepancies and stop rather than silently correcting beyond the approval.
