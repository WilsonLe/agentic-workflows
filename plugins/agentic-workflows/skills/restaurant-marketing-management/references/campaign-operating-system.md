# Campaign operating system

## Intake

Turn the owner's request into a campaign brief with one stable campaign ID, one primary business
outcome, one archetype, audience and occasion, operational truth, start and end dates, jurisdiction,
budget boundary, owner, and decision date. State unknowns and the evidence needed to resolve them.

## Readiness gate

Before any publication-ready state, verify:

1. item name, ingredients or inclusions, price, terms, dates, quantity or time limits, eligible
   channels, destination, and call to action;
2. inventory, kitchen capacity, service capacity, order or booking path, staff brief, and exception
   handling;
3. every savings, scarcity, dietary, allergen, origin, sustainability, popularity, award, and
   testimonial claim against a named source;
4. consent, sender identity, unsubscribe and suppression for direct marketing;
5. approvals, measurement baseline, attribution method, monitoring owner, pause conditions, and
   expiry actions.

If a gate is unresolved, keep the plan `draft` or `blocked`. Do not weaken the gate by rewriting an
unknown as marketing language.

## Phases

- `prepare`: verify truth, capacity, economics, assets, destination, staff brief, and measurement.
- `announce`: introduce the proposition with the clearest proof and action.
- `sustain`: vary proof, occasion, format, or audience while monitoring fatigue and fulfillment.
- `last_call`: use truthful time or stock limits only.
- `expire`: remove or update every time-limited surface and stop ineligible sends or spend.
- `learn`: close the result record and decide stop, repeat, revise, or test.

Each deliverable needs a channel, phase, owner, exact destination, status, expiry date where
applicable, and its own approval target.
