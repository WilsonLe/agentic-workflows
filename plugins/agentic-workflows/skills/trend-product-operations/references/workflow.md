# Drop operations

A launch packet may include title, description, SEO, captions, short-video
scripts, UGC prompt, claims checklist, variants, price assumptions, margin,
fulfilment readiness, approvals, measurement plan, and expiry actions.

Unknown margin, rights, fulfilment, or production readiness blocks launch.
Store baselines must identify source and observation window. Results distinguish
observed sales/returns/spend from calculated conversion/contribution and
inferred trend impact. Recommend scale, revise, watch, or retire; never claim
causation from engagement alone.
