# Investment research memo

Reuse the project's existing research record when it owns these facts. This is a
fallback reporting shape, not a new state store or machine-validated schema. Follow
the [investment research method](../references/investment-research.md); fill unknowns
and unavailable evidence explicitly, and omit inapplicable lenses with a reason.

## Brief and evidence boundary

- Memo/version, date and coordinator:
- Question, intended reader and deliverable:
- Instruments, venue, currency and holding/review horizon:
- As-of cutoff with timezone; source windows and freshness criteria:
- Permitted work, selected lenses and research/debate budget:
- Benchmark, constraints and authorized holdings/cash snapshot identity, if any:
- Research completion or blocker; conclusions limited by missing inputs:
- Evidence class actually observed; empirical experiment identity, if applicable:
- Skill/prompt/model/provider/configuration and source-snapshot identities:

## Opposing cases and synthesis

- Strongest bull case, mechanism, catalysts, horizon and source IDs:
- Strongest bear case and response to the bull case, with source IDs:
- Bull response to the strongest opposing evidence:
- Decisive disputes, evidence resolving them and unresolved dissent:
- Provisional thesis and alternatives, including waiting/making no change:
- Valuation or price levels, only with method, dated inputs, units and sensitivity:

## Next research action and outcome follow-up

- Next permitted research step, owner, missing evidence and smallest repair:
- Falsifiable hypothesis handed to the experiment brief, if requested:
- Frozen decision version, benchmark, horizon and evaluation convention:
- Resume point and input changes requiring downstream refresh:
- Later outcome/observed-at identity and lesson (separate from original decision):
- Pending/failed observations and evaluation limitations, including model hindsight:

Memo completion, analyst agreement and a directional view do not establish strategy
performance, authorize orders/capital, or change trading-enabled state. Decision
diagnostics, costed simulations, paper fills and live results remain distinct.
