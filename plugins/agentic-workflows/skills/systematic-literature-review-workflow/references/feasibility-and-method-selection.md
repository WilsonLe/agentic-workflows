# Feasibility and method selection

Record purpose, stakeholders, discipline, question, evidence type, intended use, timeframe,
resources, database and full-text access, publication/integrity policy, team roles, qualifications,
conflicts, and institutional or AI-use constraints before selecting a label.

## Routing matrix

| Need | Candidate family | Conduct guidance to select | Reporting guidance to consider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intervention/effectiveness | Systematic review | Domain manual such as Cochrane or JBI | PRISMA 2020 |
| Etiology/risk, diagnosis, prognosis, prevalence, economics, textual evidence | Specialist systematic review | Design/domain-specific manual | PRISMA 2020 plus applicable extension |
| Qualitative findings | Qualitative evidence synthesis | Selected qualitative synthesis manual | PRISMA 2020 and method-specific guidance |
| Mixed evidence | Mixed-methods systematic review | Selected mixed-methods manual | PRISMA 2020 and method-specific guidance |
| Software-engineering evidence | Software-engineering SLR | Kitchenham/Charters or current field guidance | Field guidance plus applicable PRISMA items |
| Topic structure and research gaps | Systematic mapping | Field-specific mapping guidance | Method-specific reporting |
| Broad concepts/evidence types | Scoping review | JBI or other justified scoping manual | PRISMA-ScR |
| Time-constrained decision | Rapid review | Explicit rapid-review method and shortcuts | PRISMA 2020 with every shortcut disclosed |
| Reviews as the unit | Umbrella review/overview | Domain overview method | PRIOR or other applicable guidance |
| Ordinary interpretive overview | Narrative/integrative review | Separate non-systematic capability | Do not label systematic |

Store `review_family`, `question_framework`, `conduct_guidance`, `reporting_guidance`, and
`rationale` separately. Unsupported combinations, insufficient access, or an absent required human
team block protocol approval. Constraints may justify a different review family; they do not
justify hiding shortcuts.
