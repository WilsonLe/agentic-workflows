# Synthesis and certainty

Before combining evidence, record clinical or domain, conceptual, methodological, and statistical
compatibility. Identify the question/outcome/finding, included reports/studies, planned versus
post-hoc status, grouping and prioritization, missing-data handling, transformations, sensitivity,
subgroups, reporting-bias work, limitations, and human verification.

Meta-analysis is optional and outside the bundled CLI. For an external result preserve tool,
package and version, code/command reference, exact input and output hashes, run environment,
diagnostics, model/effect measure/heterogeneity settings, and a named qualified human verification.
The validator checks provenance, not statistical correctness. Incompatible evidence blocks pooling.

Without meta-analysis, name and justify an explicit synthesis method. Avoid vote counting by
statistical significance and vague “narrative synthesis.” Use SWiM reporting fields when applicable;
SWiM does not itself choose the synthesis method. Qualitative and mixed-methods reviews use their
selected conduct guidance.

Certainty is framework-, outcome-, and finding-specific. Apply GRADE, GRADE-CERQual, or another
justified framework only when applicable; record starting point, domain judgments, reasons,
evidence references, decision makers, conflicts, and final category. Omit rather than fabricate a
certainty rating.
