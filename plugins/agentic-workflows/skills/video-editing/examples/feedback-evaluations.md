# Feedback and revision evaluations

Rehearse the sequence through the linked owners and record the resulting decision,
artifact delta and evidence limit. These are redacted workflow cases, not claims
that an agent has produced a pleasing cut or that one creative style suits all work.

| Request / state | Expected decision and observable evidence |
| --- | --- |
| An energetic reel is accepted, then mixed playback speeds are rejected. | The existing checkpoint retains energy and records 1× footage. Source trims fit the accepted output schedule; changed ranges are checked while grade, framing, duration and unaffected shots are preserved. A short source handle triggers another selection or an explicit tradeoff. |
| A music-synced cut receives requests for fewer repeats, longer holds and less panning. | Review semantic activity labels across the entire cut, including different angles/files of one event. Select steadier ranges and phrase/section accents; do not restore short holds merely to hit every beat. Inspect neighboring frames for subject-position jumps. |
| A mostly dark long clip has sparse contact-sheet samples, but the brief calls for night activity. | Coverage is uncertain. Inspect relevant ranges more densely and test exposure on an analysis copy before excluding the whole clip. Document usable ranges or the unresolved search limit, and account for night coverage in the storyboard. |
| A requested subject cannot be found; new files arrive; the user says to keep indexing and later drops that subject. | Index the additions against the existing asset inventory, preserve unchanged annotations/transcripts, update corrected search labels, then remove the dropped requirement. Continue the requested storyboard revision without waiting for that locator or claiming the subject never existed. |
| A user spots a repeated activity twice, then corrects the count to three and asks for one appearance. | Recount the whole storyboard by activity, not file ID. Keep one purposeful occurrence and replace every other appearance; verify the total before reporting completion. |
| A sung track gets successive requests for fewer sentences, music-led pacing, abstract lyrics and then more meaning. | Music Composition's revision method keeps sparse phrases, fillers and instrumental space while making text alternatives more context-grounded. A lyric-first instruction pauses take generation. Prior selection does not prevent later lyric correction. TTS access alone is not singing capability. |
| The user requests finite-pose jiggly graphics, then whole-scene lifetime, then a smaller and less realistic shape. | Motion Design consumes the scene interval and derives pose deterministically from local frame. Preserve the accepted lifetime and loop while changing scale and artwork separately. Inspect encoded first/middle/last and adjacent excluded frames, one-cycle repeat and clearance from moving subjects. |
| A sprite render fails, or a second chat resumes with an older preview. | Preserve the previous master, use the current timeline/checkpoint and resume the failed stage. A storyboard or old preview does not become a verified current export; a passed decode does not become listening approval. |

Video Editing owns the timeline/checkpoint, editorial index and final assembly.
Music Composition owns lyric/score iteration; Motion Design owns seekable graphics.
Use their existing references and helpers rather than adding a parallel ledger,
provider wrapper, validator or rendering engine for these cases.
