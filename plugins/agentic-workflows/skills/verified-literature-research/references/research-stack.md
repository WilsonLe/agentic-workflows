# Codex research stack

Use the tools exposed in the current Codex session and read their schemas before
use. Search scholarly databases, publisher sites, arXiv, Crossref and Semantic
Scholar for discovery and bibliographic verification. Prefer connected sources
when they supply the authorized paper or metadata.

Download authorized PDFs to the paper's local folder, confirm title/authors/year
and DOI/version, compute the evidence hash and extract text using available PDF
or OCR tools. Inspect pages when extraction loses figures, equations or layout.
Full-paper reading and local evidence are separate from search snippets or metadata.

Load the installed `humanizer` skill for the prose pass and recheck citations and
technical claims afterward. Academic and literature-review workflows retain their
own scope, verification and drafting gates.

arXiv is a preprint repository, not proof of peer review. Citation graphs aid
discovery; DOI/publisher metadata verifies published records. Missing access or
PDF/OCR tools must remain explicit evidence gaps.
