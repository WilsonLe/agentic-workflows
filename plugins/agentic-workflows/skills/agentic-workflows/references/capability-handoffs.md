## Working across capabilities

Choose the smallest set of workflows that can finish the user's task. This map
is discovery, not proof that a tool is installed or an instruction to load every
skill. Follow the chosen skill's context-reuse rule for its entrypoint and needed
references, then check its prerequisites against the current host. For external
plugins, use the host's actual skill/tool discovery; this repository cannot promise
their availability.

Carry a concrete handoff: the user's objective and authority, accepted constraints,
input paths and provenance, output format, state/timing owner, and checks already
performed or still unobserved. The receiving workflow returns artifact paths,
changes to shared inputs, and evidence with its limits. Continue in the same task;
starting or messaging another task needs the applicable authorization. Preserve
the current delivery workflow for tracked changes and external writes.

Useful combinations:

- Research and writing: literature workflows find and verify sources; academic
  writing assembles the evidence; guided writing and Humanizer revise prose while
  preserving the selected workflow's citation and authorship constraints.
- Finance research: Finance and Trading Research (`trading-strategy-research`)
  owns dated investment memos, analyst/opposing-case/risk synthesis and frozen
  stock/futures experiment evidence. Pass the brief, cutoff/source identities,
  authorized portfolio constraints and unresolved dissent; for measured experiments
  include code/config, costs, all-trial history and evaluation exposure. Literature
  research supplies verified papers; engineering uses the project's existing engine.
  Memos and historical validation do not grant brokerage or live-trading authority.
  Budgeting/accounting workflows retain their account and ledger operations.
- Visual media: UI Design owns interactive interfaces; Motion Design owns rendered
  animation and scene timing; Music Composition supplies original instrumental
  music and timed cues. Discover an available
  image-generation/editing capability when raster assets are needed.
- Engineering and providers: engineering workflows diagnose, explore, implement,
  and review under the repository's delivery rules. Use Standard Development
  Workflow in Codex. Ponytail supplies
  the smallest-complete-change ladder for implementation
  and dependency choices inside those workflows; it does not add another review or
  replace the architecture owner. Provider skills operate the
  selected service after checking credentials and the exact target. Modern Web
  Guidance and Chrome Extensions handle their respective platform contracts.
- Media research: YouTube inspection, library sync, and media operations separate
  source discovery, indexed records, and authorized media retrieval. Pass actual
  assets and provenance to creative workflows; retrieval is not a music license.
- Business tasks: Trend to Product workflows share their workspace and staged
  evidence. Marketing and restaurant workflows consume supported briefs.
- Instruction maintenance: Agent Instruction Design owns instruction changes;
  onboarding concerns installed state. Configuration transfer requires an installed
  skill in Codex. Skill discovery
  never widens authority to orchestration, paid tools, deployment, or publishing.
