# Humanization and academic integrity

## Mandatory order

Apply the prose pass after factual and citation checks. Style edits must not become evidence edits.

1. Verify claims, quotations, figures, names, dates, identifiers, and citations.
2. Load the installed humanizer skill if available.
3. Humanize the prose while preserving meaning and disciplinary precision.
4. Recheck every edited sentence that contains a citation, quotation, number, technical term, or qualified claim.
5. Record the pass in the stage artifact.

## Humanization checklist

- Match a supplied writing sample when the task permits it.
- Prefer concrete subjects and active constructions where academically appropriate.
- Vary sentence length naturally without forcing fragments.
- Remove inflated significance, generic scene-setting, vague authorities, promotional language, filler, repetitive conclusions, excessive signposting, synonym cycling, and formulaic three-part lists.
- Keep necessary disciplinary hedging. Do not turn “may,” “suggests,” or “under these conditions” into certainty.
- Keep terminology stable. Do not swap technical terms merely to avoid repetition.
- Keep headings and structure appropriate to the task sheet, even if a generic humanizer would simplify them.

## Immutable content

Do not change:

- direct quotations;
- source titles, author names, DOI or arXiv identifiers;
- data, calculations, statistical results, dates, or page locators;
- citation keys and bibliography fields;
- task-sheet wording that must be reproduced;
- the meaning or confidence level of a source claim;
- required AI-use, authorship, ethics, or conflict declarations.

## Integrity boundaries

- Humanization is not detector evasion.
- Follow the institution's declared AI policy and the user's truthful disclosure obligations.
- Do not imitate a named living person's style unless the user supplied their own sample and asked to match their voice.
- Do not invent personal reflection, workplace experience, interviews, survey data, laboratory results, software output, screenshots, or analysis.
- If a task requires the student's own reasoning, treat generated prose as a draft for the student to verify and revise.
- If the task sheet prohibits generative AI, stop before producing prohibited content and offer permitted support.

## Stage record

Append this to each deliverable:

```markdown
## Quality record

- Humanizer: [skill name and version, or fallback checklist]
- Voice sample used: [yes/no; source]
- Claim/citation recheck: [pass/fail]
- Integrity constraints: [summary]
- Outstanding issues: [none or list]
```
