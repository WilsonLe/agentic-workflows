# Trend Product Discovery

Read [evidence and scoring](references/workflow.md). Use project records rather
than chat history. Require independent signal families for launch decisions and
keep observed coverage separate from the requested demographic. Persist source
IDs, timestamps, metrics with units/windows, observation versus inference,
conflicts, exclusions, confidence, and limitations. A popular item is evidence,
not proof of purchase intent or profitability.
