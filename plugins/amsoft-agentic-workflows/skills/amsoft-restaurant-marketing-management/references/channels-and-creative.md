# Channels and creative

Assign each channel one job in the guest journey.

- Owned menu or landing page: authoritative facts, terms, price, dates, and conversion destination.
- Local listing: discovery, current hours, menu path, and high-intent action.
- Organic social: visual introduction, proof, occasion, reminders, and last call.
- Email, SMS, or push: consented audience, relevant reason to return, direct action, and clear opt-out.
- In-store: decision-point explanation and staff-supported conversion.
- Paid media: bounded audience and objective with spend, attribution, and pause controls.
- Influencer or creator: truthful experience, written scope, compensation disclosure, usage rights,
  and no scripted false opinion.

Build the message from a verified promise, supporting proof, and one CTA. Adapt format and context to
the channel rather than duplicating one caption everywhere. Every asset must preserve the same
truth-critical price, dates, terms, availability, and destination.

Use food imagery only when it represents the actual dish. Route curation, crop, correction, or
non-generative food-photo work to `food-image-editing`. Do not use synthetic food to prove what a
guest will receive.
