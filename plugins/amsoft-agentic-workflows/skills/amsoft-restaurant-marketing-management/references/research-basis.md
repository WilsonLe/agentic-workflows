# Research basis

The operating model was designed from primary and authoritative guidance available during the
2026-07-29 research pass. Live campaigns must refresh drift-sensitive rules.

- Google Business Profile guidance on representing a business accurately, maintaining current
  information, and prohibited or restricted content.
- Google Maps user-contributed-content policy on fake engagement, review manipulation, and
  incentivized or selectively solicited reviews.
- Australian Competition and Consumer Commission guidance on advertising, promotions, reviews,
  testimonials, prices, and misleading claims.
- Australian Communications and Media Authority guidance on consent, sender identification,
  unsubscribe, and commercial electronic messages.
- Australian Privacy Principles and direct-marketing guidance from the Office of the Australian
  Information Commissioner.
- Australian Association of National Advertisers advertising codes, including distinguishable
  advertising and food, beverage, alcohol, and children-related considerations.
- US Federal Trade Commission endorsement guidance as an additional influencer-disclosure reference.
- Google Analytics campaign URL builder conventions for UTM parameters.

These sources inform gates and prohibitions; they do not supply restaurant-specific facts,
economics, customer consent, or proof that a campaign caused an outcome.
