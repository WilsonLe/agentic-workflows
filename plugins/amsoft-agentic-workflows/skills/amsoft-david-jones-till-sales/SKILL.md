# David Jones Till Sales

Use this as a practical, one-transaction-at-a-time till checklist. The working
name for this process is **Till Sale — EFTPOS (No Cash)**. It is based on the
operator's supplied training notes, not a claim that this is David Jones'
official process name or a universal POS configuration.

## Operating boundaries

- Treat the current store's training, manager instructions, and on-screen till
  prompts as authoritative if they differ from this guide.
- Use only an authorized till. The operator enters their own employee number
  and password; never ask for, repeat, record, or store either credential.
- Ask for Rewards or a phone number discreetly and only for this transaction.
  Do not guess a customer, create an account, expose another customer's data,
  or retain a phone number in notes.
- Remove security or paper tags only when the merchandise and current store
  procedure authorize it. Never bypass a security control or detag an item
  outside the active sale.
- This first release covers the EFTPOS **no-cash** path described below. It
  does not cover cash, refunds, returns, exchanges, split tender, gift cards,
  discounts, price overrides, delivery orders, or other exception workflows.
- If the till, detagger, or payment terminal does not behave as expected,
  pause the sale and follow the current store escalation procedure. Do not
  invent a workaround or claim that payment succeeded.

## Fast path

Keep the customer informed while waiting for the till or terminal. When the
operator asks for the process one step at a time, give the next step and wait
for confirmation before continuing.

### 1. Prepare the sale and sign in

1. Receive the customer's items at the till and keep the items together.
2. Sign in to the authorized till with the operator's employee number and
   password. Shield the screen and keypad as appropriate; do not disclose the
   credentials to the customer or assistant.

### 2. Look up Rewards, without blocking the sale

Ask:

> Do you have your David Jones Rewards card with you?

- If yes, scan or enter it using the till's current prompt and select the
  correct customer profile.
- If no, politely ask whether they would like to try the phone number on the
  Rewards account. Search the number in the till and select the matching
  customer only when the till returns one.
- If the customer declines, the number does not return a match, or the lookup
  is unavailable, say **“No worries, we can continue without it.”** Do not
  delay the sale or make up a result.

### 3. Scan every item

Scan each item one by one. Allow the till a moment to refresh and display the
item before scanning the next one. Check the displayed item and quantity as it
appears so a slow refresh is not mistaken for a failed scan or duplicated scan.

If a barcode does not register, the till shows an unexpected item, or the
quantity is wrong, stop and use the current approved lookup or supervisor
procedure before finalizing the sale.

### 4. Detag before finalizing

Before taking payment, inspect the items for tags that the current store
procedure requires you to remove:

- Use the approved detagging machine for a security tag.
- Use the approved process for a paper tag.
- Wait for the detagging machine's confirmation beep when one is expected.
- Keep the detagged items with the active sale and confirm no required tag was
  missed before handing anything over.

If the detagger does not beep or the tag will not release, stop and ask the
supervisor or follow the store's approved recovery path. Do not force the
machine or hand over an item whose required tag has not been handled.

### 5. Total and choose EFTPOS with no cash

1. Confirm the item count and visible total with the customer before payment.
2. Press **Total**. The till should show the payment choices.
3. On the left-hand side, choose the **Card / EFTPOS — No Cash** option named
   by the current till, then press **Enter**. Exact wording or placement may
   vary by store configuration.
4. Check that the tapping/payment terminal is ready, then invite the customer
   to tap or otherwise follow the terminal's supported contactless prompt. Do
   not handle the customer's card or ask for a PIN.
5. Wait for the terminal and till to show a completed or approved payment.
   Approval is not established merely because the customer tapped.

If the terminal is not ready, the payment is declined, the customer cancels,
or the till and terminal disagree, keep the transaction open and follow the
current store procedure or call a supervisor. Do not press through an
uncertain result or run a second payment without confirming the first result.

### 6. Continue and print the receipt

After the terminal completes successfully, press the till's **Continue** button
when it appears to proceed to receipt printing. Confirm that the receipt has
printed before packing the order. If it does not print or the till remains in
an uncertain state, pause and follow the store's receipt-recovery procedure.

### 7. Offer a bag and hand over the purchase

Ask:

> Would you like a bag?

If the customer wants one:

1. Fold the receipt in half and show the customer the David Jones side of the
   receipt.
2. Choose a bag of a suitable size.
3. Put the folded receipt into the bag.
4. Put the items into the bag without damaging or obscuring them.
5. Hand the bag to the customer.

If they do not want a bag, hand over the items and receipt using the current
store presentation practice.

Close with a friendly farewell such as:

> Thank you. Have a good day.

## Quick exception routing

| Situation | Safe next action |
| --- | --- |
| Rewards card or phone lookup has no match | Say “No worries,” continue without attaching a customer profile, and do not retain the number. |
| Till is slow after a scan | Wait for the item to appear before scanning again; check quantity and description. |
| Barcode, quantity, or price is unexpected | Stop before total and use the approved till lookup or supervisor path. |
| Required tag is still attached or the detagger gives no expected beep | Do not hand over the item; pause and escalate through the current store process. |
| EFTPOS terminal is not ready, declines, or has an uncertain result | Keep the sale open, do not duplicate payment, and ask the supervisor or follow the terminal procedure. |
| Receipt does not print after Continue | Do not claim completion; use the store's receipt-recovery procedure. |

## Out of scope for this release

Route questions about returns, exchanges, refunds, cash, split payments,
gift cards, discounts, price overrides, stock discrepancies, delivery,
complaints, or other POS flows to a later skill or the current David Jones
training and supervisor process. Do not fill those gaps with assumptions.
